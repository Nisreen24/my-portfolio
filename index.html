About me

I turn complex ideas into clear, thoughtful digital experiences.

I’m Nisreen, a UI UX & Brand Designer working across digital experiences, interfaces and visual identity. I enjoy taking complex ideas and turning them into experiences that feel clear, useful and intentional.

I design for real problems, not just beautiful screens. From understanding requirements and shaping user flows to building interfaces and scalable design systems, I focus on making every decision serve a purpose.

I care about the details that make the difference. Typography, hierarchy, interaction and visual consistency all come together to create products and brands that feel considered, cohesive and memorable.
