Context

A thousand-year institution, going online.

AzharX is a new Arabic-first learning platform inspired by the Al-Azhar tradition. Students around the world enroll in structured Islamic courses taught by trusted scholars, follow guided learning paths and study with AI-assisted tools, all in a calm, modern RTL experience.

The product goals were set by the PM: bring Al-Azhar’s moderate curriculum to a global audience, turn scattered content into structured courses and paths, and support learning with modern AI tools. My job was to give that product a face. Something that felt like Al-Azhar, not like a course marketplace.

Art direction

The challenge

How do you make a screen feel reverent without making it feel old?

Faith-based education lives or dies on trust. Stock illustrations and glossy gradients would have made AzharX look like any other EdTech product. Historical photography alone would have made it look like an archive.

The answer was collage art. Cut-out photography of real scholars and students, torn paper, vintage Al-Ahram newsprint, grid paper, gold birds and Cairo’s minarets, assembled on a warm ivory ground. Heritage and the present, in the same frame.

  6. 06

Exploration

Finding the direction on the board.

Before a single screen, the direction was built in Figma. A brainstorming board gathered references in four lanes: minarets and mosques, collage composition, infographic art, and Islamic pattern, next to the Al-Azhar emblem and a first colour strip. From there a brand board fixed the palette, the fonts and a library of collage elements: torn paper, stamps, feathers, minarets, birds and calligraphy.

The screens

01 · Homepage

One home for every kind of learner.

The homepage serves new visitors, enrolled students and scholars at once. The collage hero sets the tone, then the page gets practical fast: latest courses, learning paths by level, how the journey works, why students choose AzharX and the certificate. Scroll inside the window to browse it.

Design decision

The collage lives only in the hero and the closing band. Everything in between is quiet ivory cards, so the artwork stays special and the content stays readable.

02 · Learning path

Progress you can see.

A path groups courses into units with clear states: complete, current, locked. Progress sits at the top with the suggested next lesson, and the certificate is shown up front so the goal is visible from day one.

Design decision

The dark photographic header uses the same collage material as the homepage, so a student never feels they left the world of AzharX when they enter a path.

03 · Course & AI assistant

The lesson, the scholar, and a helper on the side.

Video first, lesson list on the side, scholar profile below. The AI assistant lives inside the lesson as a panel with four scoped actions: summarise, explain this point, translate, and quiz me. It never replaces the scholar. It helps the student keep up.

Design decision

AI elements get their own soft violet, the only cool colour in the system. It is a marker, not a theme, so students always know what came from a scholar and what came from the assistant.

04 · Catalog

A library, not a marketplace.

Search, level and category filters, then generous cards with the scholar’s name and level on every one. Trust signals stay visible in the list, not hidden on the detail page.

Design decision

Cards use real photography of manuscripts and mosque interiors instead of icons or illustrations, keeping the catalog inside the same visual world as the hero.

Visual system

Visual direction

Warm ivory, deep brown, a touch of gold.

Paper-toned surfaces, one brown family for text and actions, gold for emphasis and the certificate, and refined Arabic serif typography. Corners are soft, cards are generous, and the tone stays reverent and premium.

Craft

Arabic-first, not translated.

  • RTL from the grid upLayouts, icons, progress bars and pagination were designed right-to-left first, never mirrored afterwards.
  • Long-form ArabicLesson notes and transcripts use a dedicated reading size and line-height so dense text stays comfortable.
  • Trust signals in every listScholar name, level and certification badge appear on cards, not only on detail pages.
  • Collage with restraintArtwork frames the page at the top and bottom only. The middle is calm cards and whitespace.
  • One accent per meaningBrown for action, gold for achievement, green for progress, violet for AI. Nothing else.
